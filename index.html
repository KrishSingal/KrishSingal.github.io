About

I am interested in algorithm design for problems in machine learning and high-dimensional geometry. Previously, I studied computer science and mathematics at Columbia University, where I was fortunate to have been mentored by Alexandr Andoni and Xi Chen.

In Fall 2026, I had the pleasure of interning as an Applied Scientist at AWS, hosted by the wonderful Nina Mishra and Tal Wagner. During undergrad, I interned as a software engineer at Apple and AWS, and did mathematics research as part of the SMALL REU.

In my free time, I enjoy long-distance running, reading, writing, playing music, and watching movies.
