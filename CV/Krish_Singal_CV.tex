%---- Research Interests ------------------------------------------------------
\sectionhead{Research \newline Interests} &
My research interests lie in designing algorithms to make modern machine learning systems more efficient and effective. My work has been guided by these questions \par \smallskip
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt, parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Efficient computation for machine learning systems.} Can we design fast and space-efficient algorithms for tasks fundamental to modern AI/ML systems, such as \textit{attention}, \textit{quantization}, \textit{clustering}, and \textit{graph sparsification}?
  \item \textbf{Self-improving machine learning systems.} How can we enable modern agentic systems to continually learn from their experience and improve over time?
\end{enumerate}
\\[2pt]
